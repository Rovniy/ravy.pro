# What I'm doing now

**Updated October 2026 · This page changes when my focus does, roughly monthly.**

This is a [now page](https://nownownow.com/about) — a public answer to "so what are you up to these days?". Unlike the [about page](/about), which is the record, this is the snapshot.

## Working on

* **Web projects at [Gaijin Entertainment](https://gaijin.net/)** — working in the web development team, building the web projects around the company's games. This is the day job, and it owns my working hours.
* **[Hold my Helmet](/games/hold-my-helmet)** — a co-op gladiator game for Steam, built in Unity with Mirror networking. Five weeks in: [the first devlog](/blogs/atd-devlog-five-weeks-into-the-arena).
* **[TCG: FANTASY Card Collection](/games/tcg-fantasy-card-collection)** — a relaxing card-collecting game, coming soon to Steam.
* **[Tiny Boo Movie](/animation/tiny-boo)** — an AI-animated YouTube series. Writing, directing, and the production pipeline are all in-house; the shorts keep a weekly cadence alongside full episodes.
* **Music as [Zynthar](/music/zynthar)** — the fifth album, *Bedtime Stories*, came out in October 2026.

## Reading & thinking about

* AI-content regulation landing on game developers this year — EU AI Act transparency rules, store disclosure policies, age-rating laws. It's the space my [Steam AI Disclosure tool](/tools/steam-ai-disclosure) lives in, and it is moving fast.
* Production discipline for generative pipelines: how to make AI video repeatable — shot lists, character sheets, and written craft rules instead of vibes.

## Availability

**Open to new projects — selectively.** Everything below runs alongside a full-time engineering job, so capacity is limited by design and I say no quickly:

* **[Fractional CTO for game studios](/services/fractional-cto)** — two client slots total. Fixed-scope engineering audit as the entry point.
* **[Product & engineering consulting](/services)** — a second opinion on decisions that are expensive to reverse. First call is free.
* **[Mentorship to a first IT job](/services/mentorship)** — paid only after you start working.

The fastest way to a yes or a no is a short message on [Telegram](https://t.me/xploitravy) or the [inquiry form](/services#inquiry) — I reply within 24 hours.

## Not doing

* Not taking free "quick calls" that are actually consulting — the first consulting call is free anyway, book that instead.
* Not writing production code for hire. I lead engineers; I don't replace them.
