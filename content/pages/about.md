## How I Think About Work

I don't see software as just code. A good system should solve a real problem, be understandable for the people who use it, and remain maintainable for the people who build it. I like moving fast, but I also care about architecture, clarity, and long-term usefulness.

The work I enjoy most lives between disciplines — where product, design, business, and engineering meet, and someone has to actually understand the problem before reaching for a framework.

## Beyond Code

## What I Can Help With

* **Founders & early products** — building MVPs, designing technical architecture, and choosing the right level of complexity so you ship instead of over-engineering.
* **Game studios** — Unity features, game UI, analytics, monetization, store compliance, and production tooling.
* **Real-time products** — multiplayer infrastructure, live platforms, WebRTC/WebSocket systems that hold up under load.
* **Teams & operations** — automation workflows, internal tools, AI-assisted pipelines, and improving existing systems and processes.
* **Consulting** — product and engineering decisions, backed by 15 years of shipping.

If you have an idea, a product, a technical mess, or a project that needs a strong engineering hand, I can help you shape it, build it, improve it, or bring it to production.
