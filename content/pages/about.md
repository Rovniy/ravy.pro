This site is where that work lives in public — part portfolio, part blog, part technical notebook.

## How I Think About Work

I don't see software as just code. A good system should solve a real problem, be understandable for the people who use it, and remain maintainable for the people who build it. I like moving fast, but I also care about architecture, clarity, and long-term usefulness.

The work I enjoy most lives between disciplines — where product, design, business, and engineering meet, and someone has to actually understand the problem before reaching for a framework.
